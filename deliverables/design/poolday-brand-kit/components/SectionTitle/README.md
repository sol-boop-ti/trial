# SectionTitle

A centred two-tone heading: the claim in `ink`, a softer follow-up in `ink-quiet`.

- Provide: `lead` (the claim, ends with a period), optional `tail` (the follow-up), optional `as` (defaults to h2).
- Regular weight (`section-title`, 45px). Never bold, never more than two short sentences.
- Example: lead "100M+ video edits made by Poolday." tail "Some examples here."

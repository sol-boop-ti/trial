Questions. Before the call.

What is a credit?

What the agent spends to make a video. A finished video typically uses ~$5–$25 of credits, depending on length and how much footage is generated.[verify]

What is the $600 first month?

Your first month at $600 instead of the list price: agent configured on your brand, 1-1 onboarding, first videos made together. Month-to-month after that.

What happens if I run out of credits?

Top up at 2× the included rate, or move to the next plan if it happens every month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Month-to-month, no lock-in.

Do we need a designer?

No. Teach it your brand once: logo, fonts, colors and Lottie, pixel-exact in every video.

Who owns the videos?

Usage rights to the output are stated in the terms.[verify]

# D1: LinkedIn post + video (v3)

**Posted from:** the Poolday hiring manager's personal account (founder voice). **Goal:** reach + many *qualified* leads (B2B founders, marketing and growth leads). **Every video:** made in Poolday, 4:5, readable on mute.
**Fill-in rule:** every `[bracket]` gets a real measured number from `METHODS.md`. No invented stats, customers or Poolday claims.

**The moment (checked 2026-09-24):** Opus 5.5 shipped on 22 Sep [S1], and feeds are full of "made this video with one prompt" posts, whose fine print often says hours of agent work [S3][S4]. LinkedIn added a "Seems like AI slop" button (1M+ clicks in its first month) [S5][S6]. Higgsfield just put an AI motion designer inside After Effects [S12]. Buyers are asking which AI video tool actually works. We answer with proof, not claims.

## 1. Angles, ranked (V = virality, L = lead quality, F = feasible in 48h, out of 5)

### #1 Same prompt, 4 apps (V5 · L4 · F3)
**Idea:** one B2B launch brief, given word for word to Higgsfield, Kling, Seedance and Poolday, results side by side.
- **Why now:** everyone is picking an AI video tool this month, and "same prompt, every model" posts already travel [S10]. People who watch a tool comparison to the end are buyers.
- **The brief (identical for all 4):** *"15-second launch video for [product, URL]. Use this logo and this product screenshot. Line: '[one claim]'. 4:5, readable on mute. End on logo + URL."* Test product: a consenting D2 company, else poolday.ai.
- **Fairness rules (published in the first comment):** same text, same 2 files; 1 attempt + up to 3 revision prompts per tool; ≤60 min of my time each; each tool's best plan-level default model; nothing edited outside the tool; every output shown as generated, **published even if Poolday doesn't win**; brief and files public so anyone can rerun it.
- **What to expect (say it in the post):** Kling and Seedance are **video models**: they return 5–15s clips, image-to-video from the screenshot, often with garbled text and an invented UI. No edit, no captions, no end card. Higgsfield is a **creative platform** (many models, presets, now an agent [S12]): likely the most polished visuals, which is fine. Poolday is an **agent** that edits end to end: brand kit, the screenshot rebuilt into animatable pieces, captions, music, end card. The point of the grid: *a model makes clips, an agent makes the video*.
- **Cost outside Poolday (public pricing, one month):** Higgsfield Starter $19 (270 credits) [S13] · Kling Standard $6.99 or Pro $25.99; 3.0 costs 6–12 credits/s [S14] · Seedance 2.0 on Dreamina ≈$1.29 per 8s 720p clip, plan $18 [S15]. **≈$45–65 total**, logged in `METHODS.md`.
- **Video:** **0–2s** 2×2 grid (each cell 4:5), all 4 playing, text "Same prompt. 4 AI apps." **Beat 1 (2–7s):** the brief on screen. **Beat 2 (7–30s):** each cell full frame for ~5s with a label: tool type, prompts, minutes, $. **Beat 3 (30–40s):** grid again, one-line verdict per tool, end card "Comment TEST + your URL". 40s.
- **Production:** 3 external runs (~3h), 1 Poolday run of the brief (≈7 prompts), 1 Poolday edit run for the grid (brief in §3). About 6h.
- **CTA:** "Comment TEST + your company URL. I'll run [10] B2B startups through Poolday this week."
- **Main risk:** looking rigged, or Poolday not winning. The rules above + "rerun it yourself" are the fix. If Higgsfield wins on looks, say so.

### #2 "Free launch video. Comment your URL." (V4 · L5 · F5)
**Idea:** the trend hook, made honest. It is also the **launch post for the growth-idea tool** (URL → video); until the site exists, the loop runs it by hand.
- **Why now:** "[new model] made this in one prompt" is the format of the week [S3]. Our video is made by **Poolday**, not by a named model.
- **Honesty check:** "Opus 5.5 created this…" is allowed **only if Poolday confirms it runs on that model for this video**. Unknown today → **verify with Poolday before posting**. Honest variants that work either way: *"We gave our agent one URL. This is what came back."* or *"The newest models + an agent made this launch video from one URL."* "Free, no catch" must be a real offer: **the first [20] B2B companies that comment get a free launch video**, no card, no call required. Say the limit in line 1–2.
- **Video:** **0–2s** a URL typed into a box, cut hard to the finished video playing. **Beat 1:** the homepage → brand kit pulled (colors, logo, UI). **Beat 2:** 3 style options, one picked. **Beat 3:** the final 15s launch video, then "[N] prompts · [T] run time". 30s.
- **Production:** 1 Poolday run on poolday.ai itself (or a consenting D2 company) + a screen recording. About 2–3h. Delivery cost: [20] × ~2 prompts with a saved `/launch-video` command, inside the credit cap.
- **CTA:** "Comment LAUNCH + your company URL."
- **Main risk:** LinkedIn demotes engagement bait, and a giveaway draws non-buyers. Asking for a URL (not a word) filters; unqualified URLs get a polite no (§4). Deliver in 72h, publicly, or it backfires.

### #3 Reply with proof (V4 · L3 · F4, ongoing on X, best one to LinkedIn)
**Idea:** when someone posts "AI can't make this", reply with a screen recording of Poolday making it.
- **Precedent:** Higgsfield launched its After Effects AI Motion Designer (ChatGPT plugin, "recreate a design or animation from a reference") in Sep 2026 and promoted it on X [S12]. **Unverified:** that they replied to "AI can't recreate this motion design" posts with plugin screen recordings. X was blocked here, and search found the launch posts, not the replies. Check on X before citing it.
- **Where to find threads (daily, 20 min):** X search `"AI can't" motion design`, `"AI will never" after effects`, `"one prompt" video min_faves:300`, quote-tweets of viral launch videos; Threads; r/MotionDesign "is this AI?" posts. Also the Motion/Mosaic pattern of answering threads [BRIEF].
- **What to remake:** SaaS/product launch pieces ≤30s (our buyers' world), where the claim is about AI, not about the designer. Skip art pieces, people venting about job loss, and anything under NDA.
- **Screen record:** the whole Poolday run from pasting the link (YouTube/TikTok/IG, not Drive) to export, sped up 10–30×, with a **real clock** in the corner and a counter "prompt 3/6". Keep the options-first step on screen.
- **Reply format (under 40 words, video attached):** *"Fair challenge. Poolday rebuilt it from the link: [N] prompts, [T] real time (sped up). It missed [one honest gap]. Original by @[creator]. Try yours: [link]."* No "AI can do it now" gloating.
- **Consent + credit:** credit in the reply; DM the creator **before** any side-by-side on LinkedIn and only use it with a yes; never post their file as ours or sell the remake; take it down on request.
- **To LinkedIn:** after 5–10 replies, take the best one (engagement + creator OK) and post it (copy below). **CTA:** "Comment REMAKE + your URL + a video you love." **Main risk:** reads as a dunk on a creator from an exec account. The gap line and the credit prevent that.

**Ranking logic:** #1 first, because it creates the proof (agent vs. model) that makes the others believable, and its audience is people choosing tools. #2 is the lead machine: post it 3–4 days later with #1's result as credibility. #3 compounds on X in parallel and feeds LinkedIn weekly.

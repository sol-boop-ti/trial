**Self-review, by my own pacing rule (D6):**
- **Frame 1** is a line typing on black. The payoff (the burst of shots) lands at 0:02, just inside the "hook by 2s" rule.
- **Slowest stretch:** 0:08–0:12. The asset grid holds about 4s with only a cursor moving. It should be 1.5s.
- **Strongest part for a cold email:** the personal end card ("Congrats on the $40M").

**Self-review:**
- **Frame 1** is on brand (their serif, their cream, their mark), and the line starts typing at once.
- **The meetings angle,** the reason for the email, is only explicit at 0:14. For a VP Product Marketing launching meetings, the transcript card should come by 0:05.
- **The outro runs 7s** (0:15–0:22). That's fine in a one-to-one email. For social, cut it to 3s.
